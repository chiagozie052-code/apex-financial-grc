# NIST CSF 2.0 Risk Assessment — Apex Financial Services

## Overview
This project simulates a practical cybersecurity risk assessment for Apex 
Financial Services, a fictional 500-employee financial services company 
(see /00-Company-Overview for full environment details). It follows the 
NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 to identify assets, assess threats, score 
risk, and recommend treatment — the same workflow a GRC or IT risk analyst 
would run for a real organization.

## Objective
Demonstrate the ability to identify and assess cybersecurity risk, 
translate technical findings into business risk language, and communicate 
priorities to leadership through an executive-ready report.

## Methodology
1. **Asset Inventory** — Cataloged 15–20 critical assets across departments, 
   with owners, data classification, and criticality ratings.
2. **Threat Assessment** — Identified 10–15 realistic threats (credential 
   theft, ransomware, privileged-account abuse, vendor compromise) drawn 
   from current threat intelligence sources (CISA advisories, Verizon DBIR).
3. **Risk Register** — Scored each risk as Likelihood × Impact and mapped 
   it to affected assets.
4. **Risk Treatment Plan** — Assigned an avoid/mitigate/transfer/accept 
   decision, owner, and due date to every high/critical risk.
5. **NIST CSF Current-vs-Target Profile** — Assessed organizational maturity 
   against all six CSF 2.0 functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, 
   Respond, and Recover.
6. **Executive Report** — Summarized findings, top risks, a risk heat map, 
   and recommended actions for leadership review.

## Deliverables
| File | Description |
